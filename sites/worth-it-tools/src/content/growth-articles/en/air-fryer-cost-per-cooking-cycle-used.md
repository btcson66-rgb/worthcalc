# Air Fryer Cost Per Cooking Cycle Used

> **Quick answer:** Air Fryer Cost Per Cooking Cycle Used: estimate electricity from the measured routine, actual minutes, batch size, and delivered rate instead of treating the nameplate wattage as a universal cycle cost.

## Inputs that change the result

Measure preheat, heating, fan duty, basket size, and whether the food is cooked in one batch or split into two.

## Formula and worked example

cost = measured kWh × delivered rate. Measure preheat, heating, fan duty, basket size, and whether the food is cooked in one batch or split into two.
 A 1.35 kW fryer running 4 minutes of preheat and 18 minutes of cooking uses 0.49 kWh; at $0.20/kWh that is $0.098 per batch, before ingredients. Divide by the completed cycles or sessions only when that is the question.

## Limits and common mistakes

Measure preheat, heating, fan duty, basket size, and whether the food is cooked in one batch or split into two. Actual energy changes with voltage, thermostat cycling, load, pauses, standby, water or ingredients, and the tariff on the bill. Keep ingredients, water, labour, equipment depreciation, and cleanup separate; do not turn an estimate into a product-efficiency or health guarantee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I use label watts or a wall measurement?

Measure preheat, heating, fan duty, basket size, and whether the food is cooked in one batch or split into two. A 1.35 kW fryer running 4 minutes of preheat and 18 minutes of cooking uses 0.49 kWh; at $0.20/kWh that is $0.098 per batch, before ingredients. When preheat, warming, pulses, or thermostat cycling exist, keep the kWh for the full routine instead of multiplying the rated watts alone.
