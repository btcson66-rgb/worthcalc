# Air Purifier Cost Per Clean-Air Hour Used: Compare Watts, CADR, and Filters

> **Quick answer:** Calculate a room air cleaner's cost per operating hour or delivered clean-air measure from watts, runtime, filter replacement, room size, rates, and equipment use without turning efficiency into a health promise.

## Inputs that change the result

Track operating watts by speed, hours, filter price and life, electricity rate, delivery charges, room size, CADR, purchase price, and whether the unit runs continuously or only during occupied periods.

## Formula and worked example

effective clean-air-hour cost = (energy + delivery charges + filter allocation + equipment allocation) ÷ operating hours; also report watts per CADR when comparing models. A 45-watt cleaner running 10 hours uses 0.45 kWh. At $0.20 per kWh the energy is $0.09, before filter and equipment allocation. A larger room or higher speed may change both service and energy.

## Compare real use cases

Compare sleep, automatic, and high settings and keep room size consistent. ENERGY STAR uses Integrated Energy Factor and CADR-related testing; CADR is a performance measure, not a guarantee about a person's health outcome.

## Limits and common mistakes

Do not make medical, air-quality, or guaranteed health claims. A certified product's test result does not predict your filter loading, doors and windows, placement, noise tolerance, or exact bill.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should filter replacements be part of hourly cost?

Yes for a long-run ownership view. Allocate the filter price over a defensible number of hours and show energy separately so the assumption can be changed.
