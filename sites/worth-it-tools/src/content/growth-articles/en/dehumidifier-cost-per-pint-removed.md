# Dehumidifier Cost Per Pint Removed: Compare Energy, Runtime, and Moisture

> **Quick answer:** Calculate the cost of removing a pint of water from actual kWh, runtime, rate, drainage, maintenance, and equipment use rather than using a rated capacity as your personal bill.

## Inputs that change the result

Track kWh, hours, pints collected, electricity rate, delivery charges, filter and bucket cleaning, repairs, purchase price, useful life, and whether the unit cycles off or runs continuously.

## Formula and worked example

effective cost per pint removed = (energy + delivery charges + maintenance + equipment allocation) ÷ pints actually removed. A month with $42 of electricity, $8 delivery, $5 maintenance, and $20 equipment allocation that removes 500 pints costs $75 ÷ 500 = $0.15 per pint. The result changes if the unit removes less water at a different temperature.

## Compare real use cases

Compare a damp week, a dry week, and a target-humidity setting. ENERGY STAR defines Integrated Energy Factor under test conditions; capacity in pints per day is not the same as your home's collected volume or cost.

## Limits and common mistakes

Do not copy a product's energy-saving example or treat the rated pints-per-day figure as guaranteed collection. Room size, temperature, humidity, drainage, filter condition, and runtime affect the denominator.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I calculate cost per hour or per pint?

Use cost per pint when moisture removal is the job. Keep cost per hour as a secondary view because a unit can run for many hours while collecting very different amounts of water.
