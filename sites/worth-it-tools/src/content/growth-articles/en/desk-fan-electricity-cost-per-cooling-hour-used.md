# Desk Fan Electricity Cost Per Cooling Hour Used: Include Speed and Oscillation

> **Quick answer:** Estimate desk fan electricity cost per cooling hour from measured speed, oscillation, timer, and standby energy, separating room air-conditioning and comfort assumptions. This is a cost estimate, not a savings, performance, ranking, or outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

A fan moves air but does not equal a measured room-temperature reduction. Define “cooling hour” as an operating hour and avoid claiming that the electricity number proves a comfort or room-energy outcome. Use a wall meter and declare the exact start and end. Record device mode, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff, and measurement date. Rated power can plan a test but cannot replace a repeatable reading.

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Keep fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions, and other equipment outside the numerator unless the boundary explicitly includes them. At 0.028 kWh per operating hour and $0.18/kWh, the desk-fan electricity cost is $0.00504 per hour. This is not the cost of cooling an entire room or replacing air conditioning.

Do not multiply rated wattage by the full elapsed time unless the meter confirms that load. Measure computers, displays, networks, chargers, and other accessories separately before combining them.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, measurement resolution, standby window, and failed work can change the result. Run at least three repeatable tests and change one condition at a time. For comparison, keep the tariff date and measurement boundary fixed.
