# Dishwasher Cost Per Cycle Used: Count Energy, Water, and Detergent

> **Quick answer:** Estimate the cost of a dishwasher cycle from actual energy, water, detergent, maintenance, and equipment allocation instead of using an appliance label as your household bill.

## Collect the inputs that change the result

Record cycles completed, kWh or other energy used, water gallons, electricity or gas rate, water and sewer rate, detergent, rinse aid, repairs, useful life, purchase price, and whether the cycle was full. Keep hand-washing time out of the cash total unless you are explicitly valuing time.

## Formula and worked example

effective cost per completed cycle = (energy + water and sewer + detergent + rinse aid + maintenance + allocated equipment cost) ÷ completed cycles. If a month has $9 of electricity, $3 of water and sewer, $6 of detergent and rinse aid, $4 of maintenance allocation, and $18 of equipment allocation across 20 completed cycles, the cost is $40 ÷ 20 = $2 per cycle. A label estimate can inform a scenario, but it is not a record of your actual cycles or tariffs.

## Compare full loads and actual cycles

Compare full loads, partly filled loads, and a month with repeated or extra cycles. ENERGY STAR notes that dishwasher energy and water use are measured using defined test procedures and that machines use roughly similar resources per cycle, so the number of dishes changes the cost per dish even when the cycle cost is similar. Treat cleaning results, time, and convenience as separate outcomes.

ENERGY STAR criteria identify energy and water performance limits, but local rates, cycle selection, incoming-water temperature, drying option, maintenance, and load habits change the amount you pay. Do not promise that a certified model or a no-heat option produces a particular household saving without comparing the same cycles and billing period.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a cheaper cycle make every dish cheaper?

Not necessarily. A cycle can use similar resources with a small or full load, so cost per dish depends on how many clean dishes the cycle completes. Compare actual loads and cleaning results.

### When should I recalculate?

How do I calculate dishwasher cost per cycle? Add the energy, water and sewer, consumables, maintenance, and a defensible equipment allocation for the period, then divide by cycles actually completed. Recalculate when rates, cycle settings, load frequency, or repairs change.
