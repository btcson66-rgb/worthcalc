# Document Camera Electricity Cost Per Presentation Hour Used: Include Lamp and Computer

> **Quick answer:** Define whether the result is document-camera-only or the whole presentation station. Measure the camera, lamp, and any included computer from warm-up through shutdown, then divide the electricity charge by presentation hours actually delivered. This is a cost estimate, not a saving, performance, safety, health, or income guarantee.

## Inputs that change the answer

Record camera model, lamp or LED state, autofocus, capture resolution, connected laptop, warm-up minutes, live presentation minutes, cool-down, measured kWh, tariff, and date. Count a presentation hour as an hour of live use, not room booking time. Test lamp-off, lamp-on, and idle-after-use states where the device supports them. If the camera feeds a computer, either measure the computer separately or publish a clearly labeled station total. ENERGY STAR categories and power-management guidance provide context, not a universal wattage for this device.

## Formula and measured example

cost per presentation hour = measured kWh for the selected boundary × delivered rate ÷ live presentation hours. A camera boundary using 0.03 kWh for a 2-hour session at 0.20 per kWh costs 0.006 for the session, or 0.003 per presentation hour. A computer-inclusive number must use the larger measured boundary.

## Limits and common mistakes

Lamp brightness, autofocus, image processing, USB power, computer sleep, room light, document changes, and forgotten standby alter the measurement. Electricity cost does not establish image quality, teaching outcome, or equipment life. 先固定費率、設備邊界、開始與結束時間，再做至少三次可重複的實測；若需要比較，保留原始 kWh 與量測日期。

## Frequently asked question

### Is a document camera the same as a webcam for cost?

Answer: Not automatically. A document camera may add lamp, focus, and warm-up loads, so measure its presentation boundary separately.
