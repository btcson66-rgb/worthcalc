# E-Reader Electricity Cost Per Reading Hour Used: Count Charging Energy

> **Quick answer:** Calculate e-reader electricity cost per reading hour from measured charging energy and actual reading hours, keeping screen light, Wi-Fi, and replacement costs separate. This is a cost estimate, not a savings, performance, ranking, or outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

The device may draw power mostly while charging, not during a reading hour. Use a complete charge boundary and divide by observed reading time rather than assuming a battery capacity equals wall energy. Use a wall meter and declare the exact start and end. Record device mode, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff, and measurement date. Rated power can plan a test but cannot replace a repeatable reading.

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Keep fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions, and other equipment outside the numerator unless the boundary explicitly includes them. A 0.018 kWh charge at $0.18/kWh costs $0.00324. If it supports 36 observed reading hours, the electricity allocation is $0.00009 per reading hour; this excludes the device and content price.

Do not multiply rated wattage by the full elapsed time unless the meter confirms that load. Measure computers, displays, networks, chargers, and other accessories separately before combining them.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, measurement resolution, standby window, and failed work can change the result. Run at least three repeatable tests and change one condition at a time. For comparison, keep the tariff date and measurement boundary fixed.
