# Electric Air Pump Electricity Cost Per Inflation Session Used: Include Pressure Cutoff

> **Quick answer:** Define one inflation session by the item reaching the intended pressure or stopping point, measure motor and cutoff behavior, and divide the electricity charge by completed sessions. A retry is extra work, not a hidden part of a successful average. This is a cost estimate, not a saving, comfort, health, safety, performance, or income guarantee.

## Inputs that change the answer

Record item type, target pressure, nozzle, hose, start pressure, stop pressure, runtime, cutoff, retries, measured kWh, tariff, and date. Separate a plug-in pump from a battery charger or vehicle accessory if they are not part of the same job. Test a nearly empty item, a partial top-up, and a retry after a leak or nozzle change. Pressure cutoff can shorten motor time while setup and checking time still belong to the session boundary you choose.

## Formula and measured example

electricity cost per completed inflation session = measured kWh for an electric air pump × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed units. If one completed inflation uses 0.018 kWh at 0.20 per kWh, its electricity charge is 0.0036. If the item needs two motor runs, count both in the same session and record the reason. Rated power or product labels can help plan a test; a reliable answer keeps wall-meter start and end times, kWh, tariff, and date.

## Limits and common mistakes

Item volume, starting pressure, target pressure, nozzle seal, hose length, motor temperature, cutoff calibration, supply voltage, and retries affect readings. Cost does not establish pressure accuracy, safety, or suitability for a particular item. Run at least three repeatable measurements, keep the device boundary and tariff fixed, and preserve raw kWh plus the operating definition when comparing modes.

## Frequently asked question

### Should a failed attempt count?

Record it as a failed or repeated session and include its measured energy in the session result; do not label it a normal completed job.
