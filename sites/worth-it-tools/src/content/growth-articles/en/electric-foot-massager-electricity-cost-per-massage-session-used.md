# Electric Foot Massager Cost Per Massage Session Used: Separate Standby and Heat

> **Quick answer:** Calculate electric foot massager electricity cost per massage session from measured motor, heat, pressure, and standby energy, separating personal comfort claims and ownership costs. This is a cost estimate, not a savings, performance, ranking, or outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

Heat and air-pressure modes may draw differently from massage-only mode, and a session may include setup or post-use standby. Record the selected mode, duration, surface, and whether the wall reading includes a separate controller. Use a wall meter and declare the exact start and end. Record device mode, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff, and measurement date. Rated power can plan a test but cannot replace a repeatable reading.

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Keep fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions, and other equipment outside the numerator unless the boundary explicitly includes them. If a 25-minute session uses 0.045 kWh at $0.18/kWh, electricity costs $0.0081 for the session. Keep device price, cleaning, repairs, and any health or comfort claim outside this electricity-only figure.

Do not multiply rated wattage by the full elapsed time unless the meter confirms that load. Measure computers, displays, networks, chargers, and other accessories separately before combining them.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, measurement resolution, standby window, and failed work can change the result. Run at least three repeatable tests and change one condition at a time. For comparison, keep the tariff date and measurement boundary fixed.
