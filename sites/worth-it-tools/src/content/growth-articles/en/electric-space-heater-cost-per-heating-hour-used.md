# Electric Space Heater Cost Per Heating Hour Used: Compare Wattage, Duty Cycle, and Room

> **Quick answer:** Calculate an electric space heater's cost per heating hour from watts, actual duty cycle, electricity rate, delivery charges, maintenance, and equipment allocation instead of treating a thermostat hour as full-power use.

## Inputs that change the result

Record rated watts, measured watts, hours, on/off duty cycle, electricity rate, delivery charges, room size, purchase price, useful life, and whether the heater is supplemental or replacing central heat.

## Formula and worked example

effective heating-hour cost = (watts ÷ 1000 × full-power-equivalent hours × rate + delivery charges + maintenance + equipment allocation) ÷ occupied heating hours. A 1,500-watt heater on for 4 full-power-equivalent hours uses 6 kWh. At $0.20 per kWh energy is $1.20 before other allocations. If it cycles off, separate occupied hours from full-power-equivalent hours.

## Compare real use cases

Compare a small room, a drafty room, and supplemental use while central heat remains on. EIA explains the kWh unit, but your tariff and duty cycle determine the bill; room comfort and safety are separate outcomes.

## Limits and common mistakes

Do not claim a plug-in heater is universally cheaper than central heat. Room size, insulation, thermostat control, heat source, time of use, and electricity or fuel rates must be matched.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I divide by hours the heater was switched on?

For a user-facing hourly cost, yes, but also disclose full-power-equivalent hours so cycling does not make the result look falsely low or high.
