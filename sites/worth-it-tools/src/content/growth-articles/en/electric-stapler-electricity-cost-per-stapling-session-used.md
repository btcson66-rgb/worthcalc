# Electric Stapler Electricity Cost Per Stapling Session Used: Separate Jam Events

> **Quick answer:** Calculate electric stapler electricity cost per stapling session from measured trigger, motor, and standby energy, separating staples, paper, and jam-clearing events. This is a cost estimate, not a savings, performance, ranking, or outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

Paper thickness, stack alignment, trigger pressure, and a jam can change the number of motor attempts. Define whether one session is a stapled packet or a timed work block, and count successful packets. Use a wall meter and declare the exact start and end. Record device mode, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff, and measurement date. Rated power can plan a test but cannot replace a repeatable reading.

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Keep fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions, and other equipment outside the numerator unless the boundary explicitly includes them. A 0.003 kWh session at $0.18/kWh costs $0.00054 in electricity. If one jam is inside the boundary, keep the event in the session total but report it so another operator does not mistake it for normal use.

Do not multiply rated wattage by the full elapsed time unless the meter confirms that load. Measure computers, displays, networks, chargers, and other accessories separately before combining them.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, measurement resolution, standby window, and failed work can change the result. Run at least three repeatable tests and change one condition at a time. For comparison, keep the tariff date and measurement boundary fixed.
