# Event Ticket Cost Per Attended Hour: Include Travel, Fees, and Missed Time

> **Quick answer:** An event’s effective hourly cost includes the ticket, service fees, transport, food, childcare, and the hours you actually attend.

## Collect the inputs that change the result

Record ticket, taxes, service fees, travel, parking, food, childcare, refund restrictions, event length, travel time, and hours attended.

## Formula and worked example

effective cost per attended hour = (ticket + fees + travel + required extras) ÷ attended event hours. A $90 ticket plus $20 fees and $30 travel takes four attended hours, so the cost is $35 per attended hour. Leaving after two hours doubles it.

## Use a range instead of a promise

Run full-attendance, partial-attendance, and cancellation cases. Compare the event with a lower-cost activity while keeping social value as a separate personal judgment. Run low-use, realistic-use, and high-use cases. If the result changes, show the range and the assumption that changes it. Do not use the advertised runtime if arrival, delays, or early departure change attendance. A non-refundable ticket remains a cash cost after cancellation.

Keep a short use log for a month or cycle. Separate unavoidable costs from optional extras, record missed uses, and check the current terms before the next charge. This makes the denominator auditable instead of optimistic.

### Can everyone use the same threshold?

No. Prices, usage, timing, and terms differ. Replace the example with your records and keep low, base, and high cases visible.

### What should I check first?

How do I compare an event ticket? Divide all unavoidable event cash costs by hours you will actually attend, then stress-test cancellation. Recalculate when terms, price, or usage change.
