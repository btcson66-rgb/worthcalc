# Food Processor Cost Per Prep Session Used

> **Quick answer:** Food Processor Cost Per Prep Session Used: estimate electricity from the measured routine, actual minutes, batch size, and delivered rate instead of treating the nameplate wattage as a universal cycle cost.

## Inputs that change the result

Track motor-on seconds, repeated batches, kneading versus chopping, bowl size, and whether preparation replaces hand work or simply adds a powered step.

## Formula and worked example

cost = measured kWh × delivered rate. Track motor-on seconds, repeated batches, kneading versus chopping, bowl size, and whether preparation replaces hand work or simply adds a powered step.
 A 700 W processor used for 2 minutes of chopping and 4 minutes of kneading uses 0.07 kWh; at $0.20/kWh, that session is $0.014 before ingredients and cleanup. Divide by the completed cycles or sessions only when that is the question.

## Limits and common mistakes

Track motor-on seconds, repeated batches, kneading versus chopping, bowl size, and whether preparation replaces hand work or simply adds a powered step. Actual energy changes with voltage, thermostat cycling, load, pauses, standby, water or ingredients, and the tariff on the bill. Keep ingredients, water, labour, equipment depreciation, and cleanup separate; do not turn an estimate into a product-efficiency or health guarantee.
