# Freezer Cost Per Day Used: Compare Standby Energy, Capacity, and Food Storage

> **Quick answer:** Turn annual or measured freezer energy into a daily ownership cost by including rates, fixed charges, maintenance, purchase allocation, and the days the freezer actually serves your household.

## Inputs that change the result

Record annual kWh or meter readings, rate, delivery charges, cubic feet, ice or defrost features, service, purchase price, useful life, and whether the freezer is full, seasonal, or a second appliance.

## Formula and worked example

effective freezer cost per day = (energy + delivery charges + maintenance + equipment allocation) ÷ days available for household use. A freezer using 650 kWh annually at $0.18 costs $117 energy. Add $33 service and $150 equipment allocation: $300 ÷ 365 = $0.82 per day. A seasonal freezer can use a different denominator.

## Compare real use cases

Compare chest, upright, and second-freezer scenarios using the same food-storage need. ENERGY STAR criteria vary with product type, size, configuration, and defrost; a label estimate is a test-based comparison, not your exact bill.

## Limits and common mistakes

Do not divide energy by food value and call it a savings result unless you state spoilage, convenience, and capacity assumptions. Include the cost of keeping an empty appliance only if that is the decision being evaluated.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should a seasonal freezer use 365 days as the denominator?

Only if it is available and serving the household year-round. For seasonal ownership, report both calendar-day cost and cost per active storage day.
