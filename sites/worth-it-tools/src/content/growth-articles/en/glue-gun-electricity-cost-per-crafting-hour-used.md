# Glue Gun Electricity Cost Per Crafting Hour Used: Separate Heat and Idle

> **Quick answer:** Measure the glue gun from plug-in to unplug, but define which crafting hours actually receive the allocation. Separate warm-up, thermostat cycling, and powered idle from glue application time. This is a cost estimate, not a guarantee of quality, health, safety, comfort, performance, or income.

## Inputs that change the answer

Record gun wattage, warm-up minutes, trigger activity, thermostat cycles, sleep or idle time, measured kWh, tariff, and date. Keep glue sticks and a task light outside electricity cost, then report them separately as consumables or loads.

## Formula and measured example

If a measured glue-gun boundary uses 0.07 kWh at 0.20 per kWh, the charge is 0.014. Allocated across 2 completed crafting hours, it is 0.007 per hour. This ignores glue-stick cost by design. Formula: cost per completed unit = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed units.

## Limits and common mistakes

Gun size, glue temperature, thermostat cycling, ambient temperature, cord losses, idle habits, and start-stop frequency affect readings. Electricity cost does not prove bond strength, burn safety, or project quality. Run at least three repeatable measurements, keep the device boundary and tariff fixed, and preserve raw kWh plus the operating definition when comparing modes.

## Frequently asked question

### Should glue-stick cost be included?

Not in electricity-only cost. Track glue sticks as a separate consumable and combine them only when the question is total crafting cost.
