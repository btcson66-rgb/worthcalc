# Gym Membership Cost Per Visit: Include Joining Fees and Missed Months

> **Quick answer:** Gym cost per visit uses every paid month, joining fee, equipment or class add-on, and the visits you actually complete.

## Collect the inputs that change the result

Record membership, initiation, annual, class, parking, and cancellation fees. Count check-ins or completed sessions, and compare with pay-per-visit or home equipment.

## Formula and worked example

cost per completed visit = (all membership-period costs + transport) ÷ completed visits. A $45 monthly gym with a $60 joining fee and $10 monthly parking costs $655 in a year. At 100 visits it is $6.55; at 24 visits it is $27.29.

## Use a range instead of a promise

The FTC warns that subscriptions may auto-renew and advises checking terms and cancellation. Put renewal date and cancellation friction beside your usage estimate. Run low-use, realistic-use, and high-use cases. If the result changes, show the range and the assumption that changes it. Do not count an intention to exercise as a completed visit. If a class is included but never booked, its advertised value is zero for your effective-cost calculation.

Keep a short usage log for a month or cycle. Separate unavoidable costs from optional add-ons, and record why a planned use did not happen. That record makes the denominator auditable instead of optimistic.
