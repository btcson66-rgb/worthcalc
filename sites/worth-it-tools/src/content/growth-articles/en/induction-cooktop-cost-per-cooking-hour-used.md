# Induction Cooktop Cost Per Cooking Hour Used: Separate Heat, Idle, and Cleanup

> **Quick answer:** Calculate an induction cooktop's cost per cooking hour from measured zone power, pan and food time, standby draw, electricity rates, cleanup, and equipment allocation instead of equating a one-hour recipe with one hour at full power.

## Inputs that change the result

Record zone watts, active heating minutes, power levels, preheating, simmering, standby, number of pans, batch size, electricity rate, delivery charges, cleaning time, purchase price, useful life, and cooking hours that produced edible food.

## Formula and worked example

effective cooking-hour cost = (heating energy + standby energy + delivery charges + cleaning or maintenance allocation + equipment allocation) ÷ useful cooking hours. Keep energy used for failed batches or an empty pan visible instead of assigning it to food. Two zones average 1.4 kW for 20 minutes and 0.35 kW for 30 minutes, using 0.641 kWh. At $0.20/kWh, direct energy is $0.13. If a $0.40 cleanup and equipment allocation supports two useful cooking hours, total is $0.33 per useful hour, not a wattage-only estimate.

## Compare real use cases

Compare boiling water, low simmering, and a crowded batch that needs repeated reheating. The residential electric-cooking specification is still an evaluation context, so your pan size, induction compatibility, controls, ventilation, and tariff remain the relevant household inputs.

## Limits and common mistakes

Do not claim every induction cooktop carries an ENERGY STAR residential label; the residential specification page describes the programme and its criteria materials. Do not convert cooking efficiency into guaranteed savings without comparing the same food, pan, batch, and time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should standby and failed cooking be included?

Include standby when it is attributable to the cooking session, and report failed or discarded batches separately. This keeps the useful-food denominator honest and helps distinguish appliance efficiency from household habits.
