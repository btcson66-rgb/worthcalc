# Laptop Docking Station Electricity Cost Per Workday Used: Separate Display Charging

> **Quick answer:** Measure the dock and its attached accessories during a completed workday, then divide the measured electricity charge by the workdays represented by the test. A dock may power displays, storage, network, and laptop charging, so list every connected load before comparing setups. This is a cost estimate, not a saving, performance, safety, health, or income guarantee.

## Inputs that change the answer

Record dock model, power adapter, number of displays, USB devices, Ethernet, laptop charging state, active hours, sleep hours, measured kWh, tariff, and date. If a monitor or laptop is measured through the same meter, label that energy instead of quietly attributing it to the dock. Run one day with the laptop already charged and one day with charging included. Note display resolution, USB bus-powered devices, network activity, and sleep settings. ENERGY STAR treats computers and power-management modes as meaningful distinctions; your result still needs a meter reading for this exact desk.

## Formula and measured example

dock cost per workday = measured kWh for the defined dock boundary × delivered rate ÷ completed workdays. A defined dock boundary uses 0.18 kWh across a workday at 0.20 per kWh, producing 0.036 of electricity cost for that day. If the meter also covers two displays, report a desk-system cost and a dock-only reading separately.

## Compare real use cases

Compare charging-heavy mornings, steady office work, and a dock left connected overnight. Do not use the number of days the dock could have been used as the denominator. ## Limits and common mistakes

Laptop battery state, display sleep, USB peripherals, firmware, Ethernet traffic, adapter losses, and power strips affect results. Repeat normal and low-activity days and keep hardware scope fixed; this is an energy allocation, not a productivity or device-life claim. 先固定費率、設備邊界、開始與結束時間，再做至少三次可重複的實測；若需要比較，保留原始 kWh 與量測日期。

## Frequently asked question

### Should a monitor be counted as docking-station cost?

Answer: Only if your stated boundary is the complete docked desk. For a dock-only figure, meter the dock path separately and report the monitor as its own load.
