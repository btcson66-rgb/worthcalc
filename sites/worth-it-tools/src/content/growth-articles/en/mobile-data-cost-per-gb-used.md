# Mobile Data Cost Per GB Used: Compare Plan Minimums With Your Actual Usage

> **Quick answer:** A data plan’s effective rate is based on gigabytes actually used, plus device fees, taxes, throttling, and unused rollover limits.

## Collect the inputs that change the result

Enter plan price, taxes, device payment, extra-data fees, hotspot use, included GB, actual GB used, rollover expiry, and months with travel or Wi-Fi.

## Formula and worked example

effective cost per GB used = (plan + device + taxes + expected overage) ÷ actual GB used. A $55 plan with $10 taxes and $5 device cost provides 50 GB, but only 10 GB are used. The effective rate is $7 per used GB before valuing other benefits.

## Use a range instead of a promise

Compare low-use, normal-use, and travel-month cases. Keep coverage and hotspot needs separate from the data-unit calculation. Run low-use, realistic-use, and high-use cases. If the result changes, show the range and the assumption that changes it. Do not create data use to justify a plan. An unlimited label may still have throttling, hotspot, or eligibility terms worth checking.

Keep a short use log for a month or cycle. Separate unavoidable costs from optional extras, record missed uses, and check the current terms before the next charge. This makes the denominator auditable instead of optimistic.
