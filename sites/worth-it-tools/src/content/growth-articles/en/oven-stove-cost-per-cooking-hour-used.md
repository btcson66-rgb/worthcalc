# Oven and Stove Cost Per Cooking Hour Used: Compare Heat, Preheating, and Fuel

> **Quick answer:** Estimate a cooking hour from actual electricity or gas use, preheating, idle time, rate structure, maintenance, and equipment allocation; a timer setting alone is not a household cost.

## Inputs that change the result

Record cooking hours, preheat minutes, electric kWh or gas therms, delivery charges, cookware or maintenance costs, purchase price, useful life, and whether the oven, cooktop, or both were active.

## Formula and worked example

effective cooking-hour cost = (fuel + delivery charges + maintenance + equipment allocation) ÷ completed cooking hours. A period with $18 fuel, $4 delivery, $3 maintenance, and $15 equipment allocation across 20 cooking hours costs $40 ÷ 20 = $2 per cooking hour. Include preheating and active idle time consistently.

## Compare real use cases

Compare a short stovetop simmer, a preheated oven, and a batch-cooking session. Electric cooking criteria use defined events and test assumptions, while gas bills may use therms or volume; convert units using the bill or utility heat content instead of mixing them.

## Limits and common mistakes

ENERGY STAR notes that actual cooking energy varies with usage patterns and that its electric criteria cover cooktops and ranges, not every standalone oven. EIA explains that gas heat content varies by location and time.
