# Paper Shredder Electricity Cost Per Shredding Session Used: Count Motor Cycles

> **Quick answer:** Calculate paper shredder electricity cost per shredding session from measured motor, reverse, jam-clearing, and standby energy, separating bags and maintenance. This is a cost estimate, not a savings, product-performance, ranking, or household-outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

paper shredder cost per shredding session starts with a declared wall-meter boundary. Start and end with a declared wall-meter boundary. Record the device state, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff unit, and measurement date. A rating or typical value can plan a test but cannot replace a repeatable reading.

Sheets per pass, paper thickness, bin fullness, and reverse cycles can change both elapsed time and energy. A session should start when the first document is fed and end after the chosen post-use window.

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Keep fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions, and the computer or network load outside the numerator unless your stated boundary intentionally includes them. If one session measures 0.035 kWh at $0.18/kWh, electricity is $0.0063. If 180 sheets were successfully shredded, the electricity-only allocation is $0.000035 per sheet; the session total remains the primary answer.

Do not multiply a rated wattage by the whole elapsed period unless the wall meter confirms that load. If a computer, display, network device, charger, or accessory is present, measure it separately before combining boundaries.

## Compare real use cases

Test a light stack, a near-rated stack, and a mixed batch with a reverse event. Record the number of successful sheets, not merely the feed count, and do not assign the whole office circuit to the shredder.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff structure, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, measurement resolution, standby window, and failed work can change the result. Test at least three repeatable sessions, combine short runs when the meter resolution is coarse, and compare one changed condition at a time. The estimate is not a promise of savings, performance, or household results.
