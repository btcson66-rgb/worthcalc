# Portable Air Conditioner Electricity Cost Per Cooling Hour Used: Count Compressor Cycles

> **Quick answer:** Estimate portable-air-conditioner cost per completed cooling hour by separating compressor cycles, fan time, and standby.

## Inputs that change the result

A portable air conditioner can alternate between compressor, fan, thermostat pause, exhaust mode, and standby. A nameplate wattage is not the same as the kWh used during one completed cooling hour. Record room conditions, setpoint, fan mode, exhaust arrangement, condensate handling, and whether the test includes startup and shutdown. Record the device state, start and stop readings, measured kWh, delivered rate, and exact window. Define one completed unit before calculating, and list any shared outlet load rather than assuming it is zero.

## Formula and measured example

Cost per completed cooling hour = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed cooling hours. For example, 1.4 kWh at 0.22 per kWh over 2 completed cooling hours gives 0.154 per hour. The arithmetic is illustrative, not a product average. Keep the raw readings, currency, date, and rate beside the result so another person can reproduce it. A rounded value without its measurement boundary is not an auditable household cost.

## Measurement method

Use a plug meter or wall meter and record start temperature, end temperature, setpoint, compressor state, fan mode, measured kWh, rate, and exact window. Repeat after the room reaches a stable condition; do not hide the first pull-down period inside a maintenance average. If the load is estimated rather than measured, label the assumption and show how it changes the result. Repeat comparable windows, but do not erase a real operating difference just to make the numbers look tidy.

## Limits and common mistakes

Cooling performance, noise, condensate handling, and comfort are separate from electricity cost. Do not infer a personal bill from rated watts, and do not compare two units unless the room, weather, setpoint, and completed cooling definition are comparable. Keep electricity separate from maintenance, water, consumables, repair, and any claimed outcome. At least three comparable readings can reveal variation, but repetition does not turn a small sample into a universal product claim.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a lower wattage guarantee a lower cost per cooling hour?

No. The result also depends on compressor cycling, room load, setpoint, fan operation, standby, and your delivered rate. Measure the same completed cooling unit before comparing.
