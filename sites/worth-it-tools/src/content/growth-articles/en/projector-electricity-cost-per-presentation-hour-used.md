# Projector Electricity Cost Per Presentation Hour Used: Include Warm-Up and Cooling

> **Quick answer:** Estimate projector electricity cost per presentation hour from measured lamp or LED operation, warm-up, cooling, and standby energy, separating the computer and room equipment. This is a cost estimate, not a savings, performance, ranking, or outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

A presentation hour may include a bright image, pauses, automatic brightness, fan cooling, and a long shutdown cycle. Decide whether the denominator is active presentation time or the complete booked room hour. Use a wall meter and declare the exact start and end. Record device mode, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff, and measurement date. Rated power can plan a test but cannot replace a repeatable reading.

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Keep fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions, and other equipment outside the numerator unless the boundary explicitly includes them. If a session measures 0.42 kWh at $0.18/kWh, electricity is $0.0756 for that presentation boundary. If the computer is measured separately at 0.16 kWh, report the projector and complete-room totals separately.

Do not multiply rated wattage by the full elapsed time unless the meter confirms that load. Measure computers, displays, networks, chargers, and other accessories separately before combining them.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, measurement resolution, standby window, and failed work can change the result. Run at least three repeatable tests and change one condition at a time. For comparison, keep the tariff date and measurement boundary fixed.
