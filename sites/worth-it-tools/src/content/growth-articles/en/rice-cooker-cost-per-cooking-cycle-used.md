# Rice Cooker Cost Per Cooking Cycle Used

> **Quick answer:** Rice Cooker Cost Per Cooking Cycle Used: estimate electricity from the measured routine, actual minutes, batch size, and delivered rate instead of treating the nameplate wattage as a universal cycle cost.

## Inputs that change the result

Record cooking, transition to keep-warm, keep-warm minutes, rice quantity, rinsing water, and whether the pot is opened repeatedly.

## Formula and worked example

cost = measured kWh × delivered rate. Record cooking, transition to keep-warm, keep-warm minutes, rice quantity, rinsing water, and whether the pot is opened repeatedly.
 A cooker uses 0.16 kWh to cook 0.6 kg of rice and 0.06 kWh over two hours of warming: 0.22 kWh, or $0.044 at $0.20/kWh. Divide by the completed cycles or sessions only when that is the question.

## Limits and common mistakes

Record cooking, transition to keep-warm, keep-warm minutes, rice quantity, rinsing water, and whether the pot is opened repeatedly. Actual energy changes with voltage, thermostat cycling, load, pauses, standby, water or ingredients, and the tariff on the bill. Keep ingredients, water, labour, equipment depreciation, and cleanup separate; do not turn an estimate into a product-efficiency or health guarantee.

### Should I use label watts or a wall measurement?
