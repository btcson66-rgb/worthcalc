# Room Air Conditioner Cost Per Cooling Hour Used: Include Compressor, Fan, and Idle Time

> **Quick answer:** Estimate a room air conditioner's cost per cooling hour from measured electricity, compressor and fan behavior, rates, maintenance, and equipment allocation rather than multiplying a nameplate wattage by a thermostat setting.

## Inputs that change the result

Record interval kWh or measured watts, compressor cycles, fan-only time, hours, electricity rate, delivery charges, filter cleaning, purchase price, useful life, and room conditions.

## Formula and worked example

effective cooling-hour cost = (energy + delivery charges + maintenance + equipment allocation) ÷ observed cooling hours; report fan-only hours separately when material. If a month costs $86 energy, $14 delivery, $8 maintenance, and $32 equipment allocation over 120 observed cooling hours, the result is $140 ÷ 120 = $1.17 per hour. Weather and room leakage can change it.

## Compare real use cases

Compare mild and hot days and energy-saver versus continuous-fan settings. ENERGY STAR efficiency criteria are tested measures; proper sizing, sealing, filter care, and installation influence your actual runtime.

## Limits and common mistakes

Do not promise a fixed saving from an ENERGY STAR label or compare a room unit directly with central HVAC without matching delivered cooling, rooms, weather, and rate basis.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I count fan-only time as a cooling hour?

Define it. Include fan-only energy in total fixture cost, but label compressor cooling hours separately if the question is the cost of active cooling.
