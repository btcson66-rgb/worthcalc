# Slow Cooker Cost Per Cooking Hour Used

> **Quick answer:** Slow Cooker Cost Per Cooking Hour Used: estimate electricity from the measured routine, actual minutes, batch size, and delivered rate instead of treating the nameplate wattage as a universal cycle cost.

## Formula and worked example

## Limits and common mistakes

Separate warm-up, low or high setting, lid-off time, batch volume, and the difference between cooking hours and the entire meal-preparation window. Actual energy changes with voltage, thermostat cycling, load, pauses, standby, water or ingredients, and the tariff on the bill. Keep ingredients, water, labour, equipment depreciation, and cleanup separate; do not turn an estimate into a product-efficiency or health guarantee.
