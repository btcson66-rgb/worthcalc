# Toaster Cost Per Use Used

> **Quick answer:** Toaster Cost Per Use Used: estimate electricity from the measured routine, actual minutes, batch size, and delivered rate instead of treating the nameplate wattage as a universal cycle cost.

## Inputs that change the result

Count the heating minutes for each slot, re-toast cycles, shade setting, and whether a crumb-clearing cycle is part of the routine.

## Formula and worked example

cost = measured kWh × delivered rate. Count the heating minutes for each slot, re-toast cycles, shade setting, and whether a crumb-clearing cycle is part of the routine.
 A 900 W toaster heating for 3.5 minutes uses 0.0525 kWh; at $0.20/kWh, the electricity portion is about $0.011 per use. Divide by the completed cycles or sessions only when that is the question.

## Limits and common mistakes

Count the heating minutes for each slot, re-toast cycles, shade setting, and whether a crumb-clearing cycle is part of the routine. Actual energy changes with voltage, thermostat cycling, load, pauses, standby, water or ingredients, and the tariff on the bill. Keep ingredients, water, labour, equipment depreciation, and cleanup separate; do not turn an estimate into a product-efficiency or health guarantee.

### Should I use label watts or a wall measurement?

A 900 W toaster heating for 3.5 minutes uses 0.0525 kWh; at $0.20/kWh, the electricity portion is about $0.011 per use.
