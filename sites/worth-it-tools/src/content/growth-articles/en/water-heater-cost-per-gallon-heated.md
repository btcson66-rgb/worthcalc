# Water Heater Cost Per Gallon Heated: Separate Delivered Hot Water From Standby Cost

> **Quick answer:** Calculate the cost of heating a gallon of water from actual energy use, water volume, local rates, standby losses, maintenance, and equipment allocation rather than dividing a marketing estimate by household size.

## Inputs that change the result

Track hot-water gallons or fixture events, electricity kWh or gas therms, water and fuel rates, delivery charges, maintenance, purchase price, useful life, and periods when the tank serves little demand.

## Formula and worked example

effective cost per gallon heated = (energy + delivery charges + maintenance + equipment allocation) ÷ gallons of hot water delivered. If a month uses $36 of energy, $9 of delivery charges, $5 of maintenance, and $25 of equipment allocation to deliver 500 gallons, the result is $75 ÷ 500 = $0.15 per gallon. Use your own volume and billing period.

## Compare real use cases

Compare high-demand and low-demand weeks. A storage tank can have a meaningful standby component even when few gallons are drawn; a heat-pump water heater has operating conditions, backup resistance, installation space, and temperature limits to investigate. Separate water, sewer, and heating costs.

## Limits and common mistakes

ENERGY STAR uses Uniform Energy Factor and product-specific test conditions. Its criteria do not set your local price, predict every home's draw pattern, or prove a replacement will pay back after installation cost.
