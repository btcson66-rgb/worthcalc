# Webcam Electricity Cost Per Meeting Hour Used: Separate Camera and LED Modes

> **Quick answer:** Use measured wall energy for the camera, status light, USB path, and standby window, then divide the electricity charge by completed meeting hours. A webcam can share a USB port with a computer, so measure the camera path separately before reporting a unit cost. This is a cost estimate, not a saving, performance, safety, health, or income guarantee.

## Inputs that change the answer

Record the webcam model, resolution, frame rate, built-in light setting, connected computer or hub, meeting minutes, idle minutes, measured kWh, tariff, and test date. Keep the computer, monitor, microphone, and network equipment outside the numerator unless the question is about the complete meeting desk. A camera at a low-resolution preview may draw differently from one with autofocus, face framing, or an integrated light. Repeat a normal call and a light-off call, and write down whether the computer supplied power through a monitor or docking station. Rated watts are a test-planning clue, not a substitute for a wall-meter reading.

## Formula and measured example

electricity cost per meeting hour = measured kWh for the webcam path × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed meeting hours. If the camera path records 0.006 kWh during a 90-minute meeting and the tariff is 0.20 per kWh, the electricity charge is 0.0012; expressed per meeting hour, it is 0.0008. This excludes the laptop, display, broadband, and the value of the meeting itself.

## Compare real use cases

Compare camera-only use, camera plus light, and a long idle period after the call. The useful denominator is the meeting time you actually completed, not the number of calendar slots you intended to attend. ## Limits and common mistakes

Autofocus, HDR, lighting, USB conversion losses, computer power management, cable length, and a forgotten open call can change the reading. Test the same software and frame mode at least three times; do not infer video quality, privacy, or productivity from the electricity number alone. 先固定費率、設備邊界、開始與結束時間，再做至少三次可重複的實測；若需要比較，保留原始 kWh 與量測日期。

## Frequently asked question

### Should the laptop be included in webcam cost?

Answer: Only when the question is the complete meeting setup. For a webcam-only answer, isolate the camera path and report the laptop and display separately.
