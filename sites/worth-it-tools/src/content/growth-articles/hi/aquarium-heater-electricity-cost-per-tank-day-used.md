# Aquarium heater electricity cost per tank day: thermostat cycles अलग करें

> **निष्कर्ष:** यह measured cost estimate है, product average नहीं और comfort, safety, performance, health या savings का promise नहीं है।

## Result बदलने वाले inputs

Device state, start और stop time, measured kWh, delivered electricity rate और meter boundary लिखें। For this topic, record पानी की मात्रा, target temperature, room temperature, heater duty time, measured kWh, delivered rate, filter boundary और date. Define one completed unit as **पूरा tank day** before reading the meter. If the equipment has a timer, thermostat, charger, light, or standby state, write down whether it is inside the boundary rather than assuming the label captures it.

## Formula और measured example

Cost per completed unit = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed units. इस page की unit को बिना explanation के hours, days या cycles में न बदलें। यदि measured window 0.18 kWh और rate 0.20 per kWh हो, cost 0.036 होगा; 3 पूरा tank day पूरे होने पर 0.012 per unit होगा। यह formula example है, product average नहीं। Keep the raw start and end readings, the rate currency, and the date beside the result. A rounded number without its measurement window is difficult to audit or reproduce.

## Measurement method

Repeatable reading के लिए same outlet boundary और rate रखें, operating window लिखें और raw meter reading बचाएँ। Plug meter standby और control cycles दिखा सकता है जो label wattage में नहीं दिखते। Repeat the window under similar conditions, but do not erase real operating differences. Note पानी की मात्रा, target temperature, room temperature, heater duty time, measured kWh, delivered rate, filter boundary और date and identify which readings came from the wall meter, plug meter, or device display. If a load is estimated rather than measured, label it as an assumption and show how it affects the total.

## Limits और common mistakes

[object Object] Equipment electricity को consumables, maintenance, water, materials और claimed outcome से अलग रखें। Lower electricity cost बेहतर product या decision का proof नहीं है। Do not convert a rated wattage into a personal bill without accounting for run time, control cycles, standby, and your delivered rate. At least three repeatable readings can expose start-up or thermostat variation, but repetition does not turn a small sample into a universal product claim.
