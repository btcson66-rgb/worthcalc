# Dehumidifier electricity cost per drying hour: include compressor pauses

> **संक्षिप्त उत्तर:** Compressor, fan, defrost और drain-pump states को शामिल करके per completed drying hour cost estimate करें।

## Inputs that change the result

Dehumidifier compressor और fan cycle कर सकता है, humidity target पर pause, defrost या drain pump चला सकता है। Meter पढ़ने से पहले completed drying hour define करें और बताएं कि active drying time, clock time या room-humidity window माप रहे हैं। Record the device state, start and stop readings, measured kWh, delivered rate, and exact window. Define one completed unit before calculating, and list any shared outlet load rather than assuming it is zero.

## Formula and measured example

Cost per completed drying hour = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed drying hours. 2 completed drying hours में 0.7 kWh और 0.21 प्रति kWh rate हो तो illustrative result 0.0735 प्रति hour है। यह universal appliance average नहीं है। Keep the raw readings, currency, date, and rate beside the result so another person can reproduce it. A rounded value without its measurement boundary is not an auditable household cost.

## Measurement method

Start/end relative humidity, temperature, target, compressor, fan, defrost, tank/drain mode, measured kWh, rate और window record करें। Repeat करें, पर humidity और temperature भी रखें ताकि easy short run को general result न समझें। If the load is estimated rather than measured, label the assumption and show how it changes the result. Repeat comparable windows, but do not erase a real operating difference just to make the numbers look tidy.

## Limits and common mistakes

Drying speed, mold, comfort, noise और air quality अलग claims हैं। Rated watts को bill न मानें और अलग moisture sources वाले rooms को बिना detail compare न करें। Keep electricity separate from maintenance, water, consumables, repair, and any claimed outcome. At least three comparable readings can reveal variation, but repetition does not turn a small sample into a universal product claim.

## Frequently asked questions

### क्या longer run हमेशा higher cost per drying hour दिखाता है?

ज़रूरी नहीं। Compressor cycling और humidity conditions average बदलते हैं। Measured kWh और same completed-hour definition इस्तेमाल करें।

### फिर कब calculate करें?

Room moisture, target humidity, temperature, fan, drainage, tariff या filter condition बदलने पर फिर calculate करें। These sources electricity pricing, household use, product categories और spending context देते हैं; वे आपकी tariff या measured result को replace नहीं करते।
