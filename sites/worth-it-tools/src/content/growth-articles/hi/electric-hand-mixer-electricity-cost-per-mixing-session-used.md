# Electric hand mixer electricity cost per mixing session: speed को गिनें

> **संक्षिप्त उत्तर:** One completed recipe step या batch को mixing session define करें। Batch के अंदर motor runtime, speed changes और plugged-in idle measure करके completed sessions से charge divide करें। यह cost estimate है, quality, health, safety, comfort, performance या income guarantee नहीं।

## Inputs that change the result

Beaters, speed, motor minutes, pauses, plugged-in time, measured kWh, tariff और date record करें। Ingredients, washing और kitchen light को electricity boundary से बाहर रखें जब तक total batch cost न चाहिए।

## Formula और measured example

यदि measured boundary 0.025 kWh और rate 0.20 per kWh है, cost 0.005 है। एक session में यही cost है; दो batches share हों तो allocation rule लिखें। Formula: completed-unit cost = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed units.
