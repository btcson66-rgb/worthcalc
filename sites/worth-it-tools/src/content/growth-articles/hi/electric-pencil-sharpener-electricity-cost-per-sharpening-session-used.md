# Electric pencil sharpener cost per sharpening session used: count repeated cycles

> **Quick answer:** Measured motor cycles और standby energy से electric pencil sharpener की प्रति sharpening session cost निकालें; pencil waste और blade replacement अलग रखें। This is a cost estimate, not a savings, performance, ranking, or outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

Session केवल one motor start नहीं होती: soft cores, thick barrels, over-sharpening और bin clearing extra cycles बनाते हैं। Accepted pencils और auto-stop दर्ज करें। Wall meter से exact start और end तय करें। Device mode, load, accessories, completed unit, tariff और date लिखें। Rated power test plan में मदद करती है, repeatable reading की जगह नहीं लेती।

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions और other equipment को numerator से बाहर रखें जब तक boundary में शामिल न हों। यदि पाँच repeatable sessions मिलकर 0.004 kWh at $0.18/kWh लें, total $0.00072 और $0.000144 per session है। छोटे result को zero round न करें।

Do not multiply rated wattage by the full elapsed time unless the meter confirms that load. Measure computers, displays, networks, chargers, and other accessories separately before combining them.
