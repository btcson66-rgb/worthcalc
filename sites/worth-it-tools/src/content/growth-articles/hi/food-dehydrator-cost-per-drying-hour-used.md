# Food dehydrator cost per drying hour used: measure heater and fan runtime

> **संक्षिप्त उत्तर:** Wall-meter energy और इस्तेमाल की गई trays से food dehydrator cost per drying hour निकालें; rated wattage को लगातार चलने वाला न मानें。 Electricity boundary साफ़ लिखें। यह cost estimate है, savings, product performance, food quality, health या safety की guarantee नहीं।

## परिणाम बदलने वाले inputs

The useful denominator is one drying hour, but the electricity boundary must be chosen first. For this food dehydrator, record warm-up, heater cycling, fan circulation और cool-down from a defined start point to a defined end point. Keep the starting temperature, load, mode, number of finished units, and any warm-hold or idle time in the same note. A recipe duration or package instruction is not a wall-meter reading.

पहले plug-in wall meter से repeatable test करें। Start से पहले appliance state, exact end point, tariff unit और useful output लिखें। Nameplate wattage संभावित load बताता है; यह साबित नहीं करता कि heater या motor पूरे समय उतना ही draw करता है। Also save the tariff source and date. A delivered bill rate may include generation, transmission, distribution, taxes, fees, or fixed charges; for an appliance comparison, state whether you used a marginal kWh rate or allocated a fixed charge.

## Formula और worked example

Cost per cycle or session = measured wall kWh from the stated start to the stated end × delivered electricity rate. Cost per drying hour = that electricity cost ÷ the number of finished drying hours. छह घंटे में 1.80 kWh और tariff $0.18/kWh हो तो run cost $0.324, यानी $0.054 प्रति drying hour। आधी trays भरी हों तो load लिखें; इसे full-batch cost न कहें। This is an illustration of the allocation method, not a prediction for a particular model or household tariff.

Do not multiply the maximum rated watts by the whole recipe or waiting time unless a meter confirms that the device draws that load continuously. Thermostat cycling, motor load, adapter losses, automatic shutoff, and a partly filled tray can all change the measured result.

## वास्तविक उपयोग की तुलना

Fruit, herbs और dense batches को अलग मापें; moisture, slice thickness, thermostat cycling और tray count runtime और output बदलते हैं। Per-hour metric को per-batch बनाने के लिए अलग denominator चाहिए। Repeat each normal setup at least three times when the load is small, then divide the combined kWh by the number of runs. If the finished output changes, show both the per-drying hour and per-run denominators. Change one condition at a time and write it down; otherwise a lower number can simply reflect a smaller load, not a different appliance.

## सीमाएँ और आम गलतियाँ

Results vary with voltage, tariff design, room temperature, moisture, container, cleanliness, control settings, wear, and the meter's resolution. Very short motor runs may not register reliably; a grouped test is more defensible than a single tiny display change. If the question is full ownership cost, list purchase price, ingredients, consumables, refrigeration, maintenance, and time separately instead of hiding them in electricity. Electricity boundary साफ़ लिखें। यह cost estimate है, savings, product performance, food quality, health या safety की guarantee नहीं।

## अक्सर पूछा गया सवाल

### क्या अधिक drying time का मतलब हमेशा proportionally अधिक cost है?

ज़रूरी नहीं। Heater cycling average draw बदल सकती है; actual run की measured kWh लें और duration को context की तरह दिखाएँ。
