# Label maker electricity cost per labeling session used: include warm-up and standby

> **Quick answer:** Measured warm-up, printing, cutting और standby energy से label maker की प्रति labeling session लागत निकालें; tape और failed labels अलग रखें। This is a cost estimate, not a savings, product-performance, ranking, or household-outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

label maker cost per labeling session starts with a declared wall-meter boundary. Start और end को declared wall-meter boundary से तय करें। Device state, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff और measurement date लिखें। Rated या typical value test plan में मदद कर सकती है, लेकिन repeatable reading की जगह नहीं लेती।

छोटी session में thermal warm-up और cutter actions का हिस्सा printing seconds से बड़ा हो सकता है। Denominator में accepted labels लें, queue में भेजी गई labels नहीं।

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions और computer/network load को numerator से बाहर रखें जब तक boundary में जानबूझकर शामिल न हों। उदाहरण के लिए 0.012 kWh और $0.18/kWh से session electricity $0.00216 होगी; 24 usable labels पर $0.00009 प्रति label। ये केवल formula figures हैं।

Do not multiply a rated wattage by the whole elapsed period unless the wall meter confirms that load. If a computer, display, network device, charger, or accessory is present, measure it separately before combining boundaries.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, meter resolution, standby window और failed work result बदल सकते हैं। कम से कम तीन repeatable sessions लें और एक समय में एक condition बदलें। The estimate is not a promise of savings, performance, or household results.
