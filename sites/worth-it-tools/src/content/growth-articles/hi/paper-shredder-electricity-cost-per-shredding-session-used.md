# Paper shredder electricity cost per shredding session used: count motor cycles

> **Quick answer:** Measured motor, reverse, jam-clearing और standby energy से paper shredder की प्रति shredding session लागत निकालें; bags और maintenance अलग रखें। This is a cost estimate, not a savings, product-performance, ranking, or household-outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

paper shredder cost per shredding session starts with a declared wall-meter boundary. Sheets per pass, paper thickness, bin fullness और reverse cycles समय और energy बदलते हैं। पहली sheet feed से शुरू करें और तय post-use window पर खत्म करें।

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions और computer/network load को numerator से बाहर रखें जब तक boundary में जानबूझकर शामिल न हों। यदि session 0.035 kWh और $0.18/kWh मापती है, electricity $0.0063 है। 180 सफल sheets पर $0.000035 प्रति sheet; मुख्य उत्तर session total है।

Do not multiply a rated wattage by the whole elapsed period unless the wall meter confirms that load. If a computer, display, network device, charger, or accessory is present, measure it separately before combining boundaries.
