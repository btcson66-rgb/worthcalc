# Pool pump electricity cost per filtration hour: separate run time

> **संक्षिप्त उत्तर:** Speed, priming, timer और standby को अलग करके per completed filtration hour cost मापें।

## Inputs that change the result

Pool pump priming, low-speed circulation, high-speed cleaning और timer transition में अलग power ले सकता है। Denominator एक stated flow या operating condition में completed filtration hour होना चाहिए, केवल motor-on time नहीं। Record the device state, start and stop readings, measured kWh, delivered rate, and exact window. Define one completed unit before calculating, and list any shared outlet load rather than assuming it is zero.

## Formula and measured example

Cost per completed filtration hour = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed filtration hours. 3 completed low-speed hours में 0.9 kWh और 0.20 प्रति kWh rate हो तो illustrative result 0.06 प्रति hour है। यह typical pool cost नहीं है। Keep the raw readings, currency, date, and rate beside the result so another person can reproduce it. A rounded value without its measurement boundary is not an auditable household cost.

## Compare real-use scenarios

Low-speed filtration, high-speed cleaning और priming को अलग रखें। Heater, salt system, cleaner, lights या booster pump meter boundary में हैं या नहीं लिखें। Shared electricity को बिना boundary के motor पर न डालें। related measured-device guide: [portable-air-conditioner-electricity-cost-per-cooling-session-used](/hi/guides/portable-air-conditioner-electricity-cost-per-cooling-session-used/), [dehumidifier-electricity-cost-per-laundry-drying-session-used](/hi/guides/dehumidifier-electricity-cost-per-laundry-drying-session-used/), [electric-fireplace-electricity-cost-per-heating-hour-used](/hi/guides/electric-fireplace-electricity-cost-per-heating-hour-used/), [garage-door-opener-electricity-cost-per-cycle-used](/hi/guides/garage-door-opener-electricity-cost-per-cycle-used/). Each related page has its own equipment, completed unit, and measurement window; a shared kWh formula does not make the answers interchangeable.

## Measurement method

Water level, filter pressure, pump speed, timer window, priming time, measured kWh, rate और start/end readings record करें। Normal pressure पर repeat करें; blocked filter या changed valve position दो hours को incomparable बना सकता है। If the load is estimated rather than measured, label the assumption and show how it changes the result. Repeat comparable windows, but do not erase a real operating difference just to make the numbers look tidy.

## Limits and common mistakes

Filtration quality, water chemistry, maintenance और equipment life lower electricity number से prove नहीं होते। Short run से annual cost न निकालें और attached loads अलग हों तो systems को same pump न मानें। Keep electricity separate from maintenance, water, consumables, repair, and any claimed outcome. At least three comparable readings can reveal variation, but repetition does not turn a small sample into a universal product claim.

## Frequently asked questions

### क्या lowest pump-hour cost हमेशा best filtration choice है?

नहीं। Low speed कम electricity ले सकती है लेकिन same flow या completed filtration task नहीं दिखाती। Measured kWh को stated condition के साथ compare करें और water-care decision अलग रखें।

### फिर कब calculate करें?

Speed, valves, filter, timer, connected equipment, rate या seasonal schedule बदलने पर फिर calculate करें। These sources electricity pricing, household use, product categories और spending context देते हैं; वे आपकी tariff या measured result को replace नहीं करते।
