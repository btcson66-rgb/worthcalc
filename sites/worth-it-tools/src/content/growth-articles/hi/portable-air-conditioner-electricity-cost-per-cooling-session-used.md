# Portable air conditioner electricity cost per cooling hour: count compressor cycles

> **संक्षिप्त उत्तर:** Compressor, fan और standby को अलग करके हर completed cooling hour की electricity cost estimate करें।

## Inputs that change the result

Portable air conditioner compressor, fan, thermostat pause, exhaust और standby के बीच बदल सकता है। Nameplate wattage एक completed cooling hour के kWh के बराबर नहीं है। Room conditions, setpoint, fan mode, exhaust arrangement, condensate handling और startup/shutdown boundary लिखें। Record the device state, start and stop readings, measured kWh, delivered rate, and exact window. Define one completed unit before calculating, and list any shared outlet load rather than assuming it is zero.

## Formula and measured example

Cost per completed cooling hour = measured kWh × delivered electricity rate ÷ completed cooling hours. अगर 2 completed cooling hours में 1.4 kWh और 0.22 प्रति kWh rate हो, illustrative result 0.154 प्रति hour है। यह product average नहीं है। Keep the raw readings, currency, date, and rate beside the result so another person can reproduce it. A rounded value without its measurement boundary is not an auditable household cost.

## Compare real-use scenarios

Warm-room start, stable setpoint और compressor cycling के बाद की छोटी window को अलग compare करें। Same room, door position, setpoint और meter boundary रखें। Fan-only hour को cooling hour में न मिलाएँ। related measured-device guide: [pool-pump-electricity-cost-per-filtration-cycle-used](/hi/guides/pool-pump-electricity-cost-per-filtration-cycle-used/), [dehumidifier-electricity-cost-per-laundry-drying-session-used](/hi/guides/dehumidifier-electricity-cost-per-laundry-drying-session-used/), [electric-fireplace-electricity-cost-per-heating-hour-used](/hi/guides/electric-fireplace-electricity-cost-per-heating-hour-used/), [garage-door-opener-electricity-cost-per-cycle-used](/hi/guides/garage-door-opener-electricity-cost-per-cycle-used/). Each related page has its own equipment, completed unit, and measurement window; a shared kWh formula does not make the answers interchangeable.

## Measurement method

Plug या wall meter से start/end temperature, setpoint, compressor state, fan mode, measured kWh, rate और exact window record करें। Room stable होने पर repeat करें, लेकिन initial pull-down को maintenance average से छिपाएँ नहीं। If the load is estimated rather than measured, label the assumption and show how it changes the result. Repeat comparable windows, but do not erase a real operating difference just to make the numbers look tidy.

## Limits and common mistakes

Cooling result, noise, condensate और comfort electricity cost से अलग हैं। Rated watts को personal bill न मानें और अलग room, weather या unit definition वाले devices compare न करें। Keep electricity separate from maintenance, water, consumables, repair, and any claimed outcome. At least three comparable readings can reveal variation, but repetition does not turn a small sample into a universal product claim.

## Frequently asked questions

### क्या lower wattage lower cost per cooling hour साबित करता है?

नहीं। Compressor cycling, room load, setpoint, fan, standby और delivered rate भी result बदलते हैं। Same completed unit को measure करें।

### फिर कब calculate करें?

Season, room, setpoint, exhaust, tariff या thermostat behavior बदलने पर फिर calculate करें। These sources electricity pricing, household use, product categories और spending context देते हैं; वे आपकी tariff या measured result को replace नहीं करते।
