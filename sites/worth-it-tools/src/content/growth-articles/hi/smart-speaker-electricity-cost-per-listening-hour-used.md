# Smart speaker electricity cost per listening hour used: separate active and standby

> **Quick answer:** Measured playback और standby energy से smart speaker की प्रति listening hour cost निकालें; multi-room devices, chargers और subscriptions अलग रखें। This is a cost estimate, not a savings, product-performance, ranking, or household-outcome guarantee.

## What changes the result

smart speaker cost per listening hour starts with a declared wall-meter boundary. Start और end को declared wall-meter boundary से तय करें। Device state, load, connected accessories, completed unit, tariff और measurement date लिखें। Rated या typical value test plan में मदद कर सकती है, लेकिन repeatable reading की जगह नहीं लेती।

Playback level, voice assistant, Wi-Fi, updates और wake-word listening wall reading बदल सकते हैं। Continuous playback hour या clock hour boundary चुनें।

## Formula and worked example

Electricity cost = measured wall kWh × delivered electricity rate. Unit cost = electricity cost ÷ completed units. Fixed fees, taxes, supplies, repairs, subscriptions और computer/network load को numerator से बाहर रखें जब तक boundary में जानबूझकर शामिल न हों। यदि one listening hour 0.006 kWh at $0.18/kWh मापती है, cost $0.00108 है। Two linked speakers हों तो pair को साथ मापकर multi-room result लिखें।

Do not multiply a rated wattage by the whole elapsed period unless the wall meter confirms that load. If a computer, display, network device, charger, or accessory is present, measure it separately before combining boundaries.

## Limits and common mistakes

Voltage, tariff, firmware, room conditions, mode, load, meter resolution, standby window और failed work result बदल सकते हैं। कम से कम तीन repeatable sessions लें और एक समय में एक condition बदलें। The estimate is not a promise of savings, performance, or household results.
